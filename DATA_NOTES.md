# Data notes

## Editorial rules

1. Prefer Kunshan Government, Suzhou Government, UNESCO and museum/heritage records.
2. Store a source ID on every entity and relation.
3. Use concise paraphrases rather than copying source prose.
4. Label coordinate precision; do not imply navigation accuracy.
5. Treat legends and transmitted stories as narratives, not verified historical fact.
6. Do not invent a relation when the current graph has no source record.
7. A reused contextual photograph must remain genuinely connected to the entity's place or practice.

## Coverage decisions

The 212-name official register is a raw preservation/name dataset, not a ready-made visitor interface. The curated graph therefore prioritises records that can support at least one of the core interactions: place, connect, test evidence, save to a constellation, or generate a schematic route.

Food techniques can appear in both `Food` and `Traditions` only when the product and the making practice are meaningfully different records. Material outputs of crafts can similarly appear in `Artifacts` while their making knowledge appears in `Traditions`.

## Known limitations

- Some official sources name a town or declaring unit without a precise street address. Those records use a labelled anchor.
- Opening hours, ticket prices and transport conditions are intentionally excluded because they change and require live verification.
- Cached government photographs are used only for this educational prototype; image rights were not re-licensed by this compilation.
- English names are working translations and should be reviewed by a local cultural expert before public-institutional use.
